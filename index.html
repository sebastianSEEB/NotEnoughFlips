Not Enough Flips · public data

Data files read by the Not Enough Flips Minecraft mod. They are rebuilt automatically from the public Hypixel and SkyCofl APIs. Nothing here is about any player.

  • data/forecasts.json · 1 h / 6 h / 24 h bazaar forecasts (hourly)
  • data/bz/<PRODUCT>.json · 7-day hourly bazaar prices (hourly)
  • data/bz_signals.json · bazaar misprices and correlations (every 2 h)
  • data/craft_flips.json · craft flips (every 3 h)
